********************************
* StateS v. 0.4.9 Known Issues *
********************************


** The following behaviors are known: **

- Time line PDF export creates heavy, slow and ugly PDF (unlike machine PDF export),


** VHDL export issues: **

- Output generation is very partially implemented!
-> Sub-range adressed variables are ignored,
-> Variables mixing Moore and Mealy behaviors are ignored,
-> Mealy actions on memorized variables are ignored.

=> Thus currently, the only handled outputs are:
-> Moore-only, full-range affected outputs,
-> Mealy-only, unmemorized, full-range affected outputs.
